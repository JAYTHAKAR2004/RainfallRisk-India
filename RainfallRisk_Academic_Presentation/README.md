# RainfallRisk India — Academic Research Presentation Package

This directory contains the complete, academic research presentation suite for the research monograph:
**RainfallRisk India — Statistical Rainfall Research Monograph (2000–2025 & 2026)**

Website: [https://rainfallriskindia.vercel.app/](https://rainfallriskindia.vercel.app/)

## Contents

1. **`RainfallRisk_India_Research_Presentation.pptx`**
   - Premium 41-slide widescreen (16:9) academic presentation designed for graduate defense and faculty review.
   - Features 21 embedded high-resolution figures, 7 genuine spatiotemporal research GIFs, native editable tables, and complete mathematical test statistics.

2. **`RainfallRisk_India_Research_Presentation.pdf`**
   - Publication-grade static PDF export of all 41 slides for offline viewing, printing, and digital sharing.

3. **`speaker_notes.md`**
   - Slide-by-slide academic defense script and talking points explaining the statistical rigor, mathematical formulations, and interpretations.

4. **`figure_mapping.md`**
   - Complete matrix mapping every figure and research GIF to its exact slide and underlying dataset.

5. **`validation_report.md`**
   - Comprehensive mathematical and data audit verifying 100% agreement between the presentation, research paper, and Jupyter notebooks.

6. **`assets/`**
   - `figures/`: 21 high-resolution research PNGs extracted from notebooks.
   - `gifs/`: 7 spatiotemporal climatological GIFs (SDII, PRCPTOT, R10mm, R20mm, Rx1day, Rx5day, Annual Rainfall Map).

## How to View and Present
- **Presentation Mode:** Open `RainfallRisk_India_Research_Presentation.pptx` in Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides and start Slide Show (F5).
- **Speaker View:** In PowerPoint, use Presenter View (Alt+F5) to read the integrated speaker notes during oral defense.
- **PDF Reading:** Open `RainfallRisk_India_Research_Presentation.pdf` in any standard PDF reader.
